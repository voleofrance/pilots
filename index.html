F.A.Q

A simple Flight Logs APP

What is Menairó Pilots?

Menairó Pilots is a Progressive Web App (PWA) designed to help paraglider pilots track their flights, gear, and flying sites. It stores all data locally on your device.

Is my data secure?

All your data is stored locally on your device using IndexedDB. No data is sent to any server. We recommend regularly exporting your data as a backup using the export function in settings.

How do I backup my data?

Go to Settings → Export Backup. You'll have two options:

  • Complete Backup (JSON): Downloads a JSON file containing all your flight logs, gear information, settings and pictures.
  • Flights Only (CSV): Exports just your flight logs in CSV format, which can be opened in Excel, Google Sheets, or other spreadsheet applications.

To restore your data, use Settings → Import Backup with your JSON backup file. Note that this will replace all existing data.

Can I track my gear maintenance?

Yes! You can log check dates for your gliders, harnesses, and reserve parachutes. The app will show you flight hours and flights since the last check.

How do I add a new flight?

Click the + button in the Flights section. Fill in the flight details including date, location, gear used, and any notes about the flight.

Can I edit or delete flights?

Yes, open any flight entry and use the edit or delete buttons to modify or remove the flight.

How do I install Menairó Pilots on my device?

Menairó Pilots is a Progressive Web App (PWA) that can be installed on most devices:

  • On iPhone/iPad: Open Safari, visit the site, tap the share icon (rectangle with arrow), then scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen".
  • On Android: Open Chrome, visit the site, tap the three dots menu, then tap "Add to Home Screen" or "Install App".
  • On Desktop: Open Chrome, Edge or other modern browser, visit the site, and click the install icon in the address bar (usually looks like a computer or + icon).

Once installed, you can use Menairó Pilots even when offline*
